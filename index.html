What works here—and what needs the full app

Try Student, Teacher and Admin modes, create lessons, complete activities, assign courses and review sample work. Role switching is a simulation, not secure sign-in.

This version does not include the newer guided-paragraph reader, Listening Lab podcasts, translated Conversation or Live Arena. Real accounts, cross-device results, live multiplayer, recording uploads and premium AI voices require the separate server-backed app.

Do not enter real student names, passwords, private articles or recordings. Device narration depends on your browser and installed voices; some device voices may use online processing. GitHub may log visits. Reset demo clears this demo's saved browser data.
